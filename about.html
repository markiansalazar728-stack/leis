WHY WE'RE HERE

A neighborhood place for really good coffee.

Grounded Coffee Co. is a neighborhood café concept built around carefully sourced beans, honest craft, and the simple joy of taking a pause. We serve espresso, slow-bar coffee, cold brew, and fresh pastries for the everyday.

We believe a great cup should feel special without feeling fussy. Our team works with thoughtful growers and roasters to bring a little more care to your daily coffee ritual.
